# Pitch Script

Target: fit the stage time limit (check agenda PDF). Rehearse with a timer.

| Section | Time | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:30 | A story or number that makes judges feel the problem |
| Problem | 0:30 | Who, how often, what it costs |
| Solution | 0:30 | One sentence, then "let me show you" |
| Demo | 2:00 | Hand off to `demo-script.md` |
| How it works | 0:30 | U-Bot as the brain, ElevenLabs as the voice (+ AVAX) |
| Business | 0:30 | Who pays, market size |
| Close | 0:15 | Restate the impact + the ask |
