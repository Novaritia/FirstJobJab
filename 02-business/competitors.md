# Competitors — ChatCFO

| Competitor | What they do | Weakness | Our edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FlowAccount** | Thai cloud accounting, invoices, tax | Built for registered businesses; manual entry | Zero input — books built from chats |
| **PEAK** | Thai SME accounting | Needs accountant-level knowledge | Plain language, seller-first |
| **iTAX** | Personal income tax helper | Salaried focus; no sales books | Seller books → tax in one flow |
| **SlipOK / EasySlip** | Slip verification APIs | One slip at a time, no order context | Slip vs order vs customer history |
| **Bank apps** | Transaction history | Can't tell a sale from a personal transfer | Deposit classifier with chat evidence |
| **Human accountant** | Does it all | ฿2–5k / month; sellers don't send records | ฿199 / month; records come from chats automatically |
| **Excel / notebook** (status quo) | Free | Abandoned in two weeks | Nothing to maintain |

## Why can't they just copy us?
- Accounting apps are form-first; rebuilding around chats is a different product and a different customer.
- Chat + slip + deposit history per seller compounds into a credit signal over time.
- Thai chat language (slang, emojis, "โอนแล้วนะคะ 🙏") needs focused tuning.

## Status quo competitor
**Doing nothing** — until the Revenue Department letter arrives. The pitch targets that moment.
