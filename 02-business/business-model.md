# Business Model — ChatCFO (Lean Canvas)

## 1. Problem
1. No books → can't prove income; tax shock under e-Payment reporting.
2. Overpaid / wrong tax (personal transfers counted as income, wrong expense method).
3. Fake slips, unpaid tabs, unpredicted cash crunches.

## 2. Customer Segments
- **Early adopters:** Thai LINE / Facebook sellers near the e-Payment thresholds (400+ deposits, ฿1–3M a year) — most worried, most to gain.
- Later: all social-commerce sellers; small shops and cafés using PromptPay; accountants serving micro-businesses.

## 3. Unique Value Proposition
**"Your LINE chats are your accountant."** Books, tax and forecasts from the chats you already have — zero data entry.

## 4. Solution
1. Chat + slip upload → books automatically, with a deposit classifier (sale vs personal vs loan).
2. Analysis + prediction: health score, late payers, cash-flow forecast.
3. Live tax meter + one-click filing pack + Credit Passport.

## 5. Channels
- LINE OA / seller Facebook groups; TikTok seller creators ("how I stopped fearing the tax letter").
- Partnerships: banks and microlenders (Credit Passport = their lead source), accountants, **u-topia app** merchant onboarding.
- Tax-season campaigns (Jan–Mar for ภ.ง.ด.90, Jul–Sep for ภ.ง.ด.94).

## 6. Revenue Streams
| Stream | Price (hypothesis) |
|---|---|
| Free: books + dashboard | ฿0 |
| Pro: tax meter, filing pack, forecasts, CFO voice call | ฿199 / month |
| Credit Passport → lender referral fee | ฿300–1,000 per funded loan |
| Accountant plan (multi-client) | ฿990 / month |
| Micro-financing at cash-crunch moments (with u-topia) | Revenue share |

## 7. Cost Structure
- AI API usage (extraction per chat + images) — main variable cost.
- ElevenLabs voice (CFO call) — per minute.
- Hosting, storage, support.
- Tax-rule maintenance (rules change yearly).

## 8. Key Metrics
- Orders extracted per seller per month (activation).
- % of sellers opening the tax meter monthly (retention).
- Overpaid tax avoided / tax shocks prevented (impact).
- Credit Passports shared → loans funded (revenue).

## 9. Unfair Advantage
- Data moat: chat + slip + deposit history per seller is a better credit signal than banks have.
- Thai-specific tax logic + Thai chat language understanding.
- u-topia distribution (PayFi app, merchant network).

## Fit with u-topia
u-topia is a **PayFi** ecosystem. ChatCFO turns its merchants' payment activity into books, tax and credit — a reason for sellers to run payments through u-topia, with a lending / financing revenue line on top.
